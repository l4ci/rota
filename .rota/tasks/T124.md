Tracked `.rota/` content is skill-owned and never hand-edited, but some entries can't be corrected through any verb:

- `rota knowledge amend` only appends, and `rename-topic` only renames or moves. There's no way to replace a stale bullet.
- `rota milestone` has no setter for the overview text at the top of MILESTONES.md.

The `rota migrate hv` dogfood left stale hv-skills wording that needs exactly these verbs:
- `.rota/MILESTONES.md:3`: "hv-skills is a Claude Code workflow..."
- `.rota/KNOWLEDGE.md:74` and `:76`: `/hv-*` skill names and `.hv/` helper paths

Add `rota knowledge replace <topic> <old> <new>` (or edit-by-id) and a milestone overview setter. Then fix those lines through them.

The remaining `.hv/` mentions in ARCHIVE, BACKLOG, DECISIONS and item files are history and stay.
